## 1. Executive Overview

Maharashtra is one of India's leading agrarian states with 9 distinct agro-climatic zones ranging from high-rainfall coastal belts (Konkan) to semi-arid scarcity zones (Western Maharashtra) and black cotton soil plateaus (Vidarbha & Marathwada). 

This document provides a comprehensive, authoritative catalog of the **20 major agricultural, pulse, oilseed, and horticultural crops** cultivated and harvested across Maharashtra. Every crop listed below is integrated into CropGuard's multi-stage vision pipeline for automated species identification, condition diagnosis (Healthy vs. Diseased), and field-level advisory.

## 3. Agro-Climatic Zones of Maharashtra & Crop Distribution

The 20 crops mapped above span across Maharashtra's 9 agro-climatic zones:

1. **South Konkan Coastal Zone** (*Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg*):
   - **Primary Crops**: Mango (Alphonso), Rice, Cashew.
2. **North Konkan Coastal Zone** (*Thane, Palghar, Raigad*):
   - **Primary Crops**: Rice, Banana, Vegetables.
3. **Sub-Montane Zone / Western Ghats** (*Kolhapur, Satara, Pune foothills*):
   - **Primary Crops**: Sugarcane, Rice, Groundnut, Soybean, Potato (Wai/Manchar).
4. **Western Maharashtra Plain Zone** (*Pune, Satara, Sangli, Kolhapur plains*):
   - **Primary Crops**: Sugarcane, Grapes, Turmeric, Tomato, Wheat, Soybean.
5. **Scarcity Zone** (*Solapur, parts of Ahmednagar, Sangli, Dharashiv, Beed*):
   - **Primary Crops**: Pomegranate (Bhagwa), Rabi Sorghum (Maldandi Jowar), Pearl Millet (Bajra), Chickpea.
6. **Central Maharashtra Plateau Zone (Marathwada)** (*Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Jalna, Parbhani, Beed*):
   - **Primary Crops**: Cotton, Soybean, Pigeon Pea (Tur), Pearl Millet, Maize.
7. **Central Vidarbha Zone** (*Yavatmal, Akola, Amravati, Wardha*):
   - **Primary Crops**: Cotton (White Gold Belt), Mandarin Orange (Nagpur Santra in Morshi/Warud), Soybean, Tur.
8. **Eastern Vidarbha Zone** (*Nagpur, Bhandara, Gondia, Chandrapur, Gadchiroli*):
   - **Primary Crops**: Paddy (Rice bowl of Maharashtra), Chickpea, Mandarin Orange.
9. **Khandesh / North Maharashtra Zone** (*Jalgaon, Dhule, Nandurbar, Nashik*):
   - **Primary Crops**: Banana (Jalgaon), Onion (Nashik), Grapes (Nashik), Maize, Cotton.

## 4. Botanical & Agronomic Profiles per Crop

### 1. Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum / arboreum*) — कापूस
- **Family**: Malvaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 150 – 180 days (Kharif)
- **Soil Requirement**: Deep black cotton soils (*Regur*) with high moisture retention.
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Bacterial Blight*: Angular water-soaked leaf spots bordered by veinlets.
  - *Leaf Curl Virus*: Upward/downward leaf curling with leaf enations beneath.
  - *Fusarium Wilt*: Yellowing, vascular browning, and plant wilting.

### 2. Soybean (*Glycine max*) — सोयाबीन
- **Family**: Fabaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 90 – 105 days (Kharif)
- **Soil Requirement**: Well-drained medium-to-deep clay loam soils (pH 6.5 – 7.5).
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Yellow Mosaic*: Bright yellow patches alternating with green lamina.
  - *Soybean Rust*: Pustules on lower leaves shedding brown urediniospores.

### 3. Sugarcane (*Saccharum officinarum*) — ऊस
- **Family**: Poaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 12 – 18 months (Suru: 12 mo, Pre-seasonal: 14 mo, Adsali: 16-18 mo)
- **Soil Requirement**: Heavy fertile soils with assured canal/well irrigation.
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Red Rot*: Midrib reddening with white cross-patches inside split stalks.
  - *Yellow Leaf Disease*: Intense yellowing of the midrib on leaves 3 to 6.

### 4. Onion (*Allium cepa*) — कांदा
- **Family**: Amaryllidaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 100 – 140 days (Kharif, Rangada, Rabi)
- **Hub**: Lasalgaon (Asia's largest onion trading market in Nashik).
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Purple Blotch*: Sunken elliptical spots with concentric purplish centers.
  - *Basal Rot*: Yellowing from leaf tips down with decay of the basal stem plate.

### 5. Pigeon Pea (*Cajanus cajan*) — तूर / अरहर
- **Family**: Fabaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 150 – 200 days (Kharif)
- **Soil Requirement**: Deep loam to clay soils with good drainage.
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Fusarium Wilt*: Sudden flaccidity, unilateral wilting, and purple xylem streaks.
  - *Sterility Mosaic*: Bushy bunchy growth and complete cessation of flowering.

### 6. Chickpea (*Cicer arietinum*) — हरभरा / चणा
- **Family**: Fabaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 90 – 110 days (Rabi)
- **Soil Requirement**: Heavy clay-loam soils conserved with post-monsoon residual moisture.
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Ascochyta Blight*: Concentric dark pycnidial rings on leaflets and stems.
  - *Dry Root Rot*: Darkened, brittle roots stripping off like fine hair (*Rhizoctonia*).

### 7. Pomegranate (*Punica granatum*) — डाळिंब
- **Family**: Lythraceae
- **GI Tag**: Bhagwa Pomegranate of Solapur
- **Bahar Calendar**: Ambe Bahar (Jan-Feb flowering), Mrig Bahar (Jun-Jul), Hasta (Sep-Oct).
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Bacterial Blight (Telya)*: Water-soaked translucent oily spots on leaves and triangular rind cracks on fruits.
  - *Wilt Complex*: Sudden yellowing, defoliation, and vascular staining.

### 8. Grapes (*Vitis vinifera*) — द्राक्षे
- **Family**: Vitaceae
- **Hub**: Nashik (*Wine Capital of India*) and Tasgaon (Sangli).
- **Pruning System**: April back pruning (foundation) + October forward pruning (fruit).
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Downy Mildew*: Oily spots on leaf uppers with white cottony down beneath.
  - *Powdery Mildew*: Powdery ash-white fungal bloom on berries causing skin cracking.

### 9. Banana (*Musa acuminata*) — केळी
- **Family**: Musaceae
- **Hub**: Jalgaon (*Banana City of India*)
- **Varieties**: Grand Naine (G9), Robusta, Shrimanti.
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Panama Wilt*: Lower leaf yellowing, buckling at petiole, and skirt-like hang.
  - *Sigatoka*: Spindle-shaped dark streaks with gray necrotic centers.

### 10. Mango (*Mangifera indica*) — आंबा (हापूस)
- **Family**: Anacardiaceae
- **GI Tag**: Konkan Alphonso / Hapus (Devgad & Ratnagiri)
- **Harvest**: March to June.
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Anthracnose*: Black tear-stain lesions on fruits and panicle blossom blight.
  - *Powdery Mildew*: White powdery coating over flower panicles causing blossom drop.

### 11. Mandarin Orange (*Citrus reticulata*) — संत्रा
- **Family**: Rutaceae
- **GI Tag**: Nagpur Mandarin Orange
- **Harvest**: Ambe Bahar (Nov – Jan) & Mrig Bahar (Mar – May).
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Citrus Canker*: Raised, corky crater-like pustules surrounded by yellow halos.
  - *Gummosis*: Amber-colored gum oozing from cracked bark along the trunk collar.

### 12. Sorghum (*Sorghum bicolor*) — ज्वारी (मालदांडी)
- **Family**: Poaceae
- **Specialty**: Maldandi (M 35-1) Rabi Jowar (Solapur, Marathwada).
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Grain Mold*: Pink, white, or black mold growth crumbling developing grains.
  - *Charcoal Rot*: Lodging, stalk breakage, and tiny black sclerotia inside pith.

### 13. Pearl Millet (*Pennisetum glaucum*) — बाजरी
- **Family**: Poaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 75 – 85 days (Kharif / Summer)
- **Drought Resilience**: Highly adapted to arid soils of Dhule, Jalgaon, and Ahmednagar.
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Downy Mildew (Green Ear)*: Inflorescence transformed into leafy mass.
  - *Ergot*: Sticky amber honey-dew fluid oozing from spikelets.

### 14. Rice / Paddy (*Oryza sativa*) — भात
- **Family**: Poaceae
- **Belt**: Coastal Konkan (Dapoli, Karjat) & Vidarbha (Wainganga basin).
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Bacterial Leaf Blight*: Wavy straw-colored lesions progressing from leaf tips.
  - *Rice Blast*: Spindle-shaped eye lesions with gray centers and brown margins.

### 15. Wheat (*Triticum aestivum / durum*) — गहू
- **Family**: Poaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 105 – 125 days (Rabi)
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Brown Rust*: Small round orange-brown uredinial pustules scattered on leaves.
  - *Yellow Rust*: Linear yellow-orange pustules arranged in parallel stripes.

### 16. Maize / Corn (*Zea mays*) — मका
- **Family**: Poaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 90 – 110 days (Kharif & Rabi)
- **Hub**: Nashik, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Jalgaon.
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Common Rust*: Golden-brown oval pustules on both leaf surfaces.
  - *Northern Leaf Blight*: Large cigar-shaped grayish-green to tan lesions.

### 17. Groundnut (*Arachis hypogaea*) — भुईमूग
- **Family**: Fabaceae
- **Growing Cycle**: 105 – 120 days (Kharif & Summer)
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Tikka Leaf Spot*: Concentric necrotic spots causing severe premature defoliation.
  - *Rust*: Orange-brown pustules on leaf undersides without premature leaf drop.

### 18. Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*) — टोमॅटो
- **Family**: Solanaceae
- **Hub**: Narayangaon (Pune) & Girna Valley (Nashik).
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Early Blight*: Concentric dark rings on older lower leaves.
  - *Late Blight*: Rapidly expanding water-soaked lesions with white mold in cool fog.

### 19. Potato (*Solanum tuberosum*) — बटाटा
- **Family**: Solanaceae
- **Hub**: Pune (Manchar, Khed, Ambegaon) & Satara.
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Early Blight*: Target-board concentric lesions on foliage.
  - *Late Blight*: Blackish foliar collapse and reddish-brown dry tuber rot.

### 20. Turmeric (*Curcuma longa*) — हळद
- **Family**: Zingiberaceae
- **Hub**: Sangli (Asia's traditional turmeric hub) & Hingoli.
- **Harvest**: January to March (8-9 months after planting).
- **Critical Diagnostics**:
  - *Rhizome Rot (Kuj)*: Basal water-soaked collar rotting and pseudostem collapse.
  - *Leaf Spot*: Oblong brown spots with gray centers on upper foliage.

## 5. System Integration & Architecture

Every crop in this catalog is supported by the following layers in CropGuard:

```
                  Image Upload (Web or Mobile Camera)
                                   │
                                   ▼
                   [Phase 1 & 2: Quality & Plant Gate]
                                   │
                                   ▼
         [Phase 3A: CLIP ViT-B/32 Crop Species Classifier]
             Maps input across 20 TARGET_CROPS with 
             botanical prompt ensembles & visual prototypes
                                   │
                                   ▼
         [Phase 3B: Multi-Crop High Precision Classifier]
             Top-3 predictions, entropy check & abstain logic
             ("Needs expert verification" if confidence < 0.45)
                                   │
                                   ▼
             [Phase 4: ICAR Knowledge Base Advisory]
             Sourced from crop_disease_knowledge.json
                                   │
                                   ▼
           [Dual SQLite Persistence & In-Place My Farm Sync]
```
